# Case 01 — VPN connectivity
**Example ID:** INC1001001 | **Priority:** P3 | **Category:** Network / VPN

**Symptom:** Internal resources unreachable after VPN connection; internet browsing works.
**L1 checks:** Confirm affected scope; inspect client status, adapter, time, connectivity and error message; try one approved reconnect and test approved resources.
**Escalation:** Simulated L2 review of authentication logs and VPN profile.
**Fictional resolution:** Stale VPN profile recreated under approved procedure; access to test resources verified.
**User update:** “We are investigating your VPN access. Please share the exact error and avoid repeated reconnect attempts while checks are underway.”
**Closure:** User confirms access. Record cause and validation.
