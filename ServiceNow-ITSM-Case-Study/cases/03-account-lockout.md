# Case 03 — Account lockout
**Example ID:** INC1001003 | **Priority:** P3 | **Category:** Identity / Access

**Symptom:** Repeated locked-account message.
**L1 checks:** Verify identity using approved process; establish scope and time; check old credentials on other devices/apps.
**Escalation:** Identity team reviews lockout events.
**Fictional resolution:** Stored credential on old device identified; updated through approved process and authorized admin unlocked account.
**Security:** Never ask for or record passwords.
