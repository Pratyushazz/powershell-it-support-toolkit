# Case 08 — Shared application outage
**Example ID:** INC1001008 | **Priority:** P1 | **Category:** Application / Major Incident

**Symptom:** Multiple teams cannot access shared application.
**L1:** Confirm scope, timestamps and errors; link related incidents; check approved outage channels; notify incident lead.
**Coordination:** Incident lead coordinates technical teams and communications.
**Fictional resolution:** Application team restores service under change control; smoke tests pass.
**Post-incident:** Timeline, impact, confirmed cause, restoration and improvement actions.
**Sample update:** “We are investigating a service interruption affecting multiple users. The incident team is engaged; updates will follow the agreed cadence.”
